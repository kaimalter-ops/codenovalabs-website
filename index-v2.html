02

A whole team of AI agents. One result.

We design systems where specialised agents work in parallel – research, sales, support, finance – and an orchestrator merges their output into one decision, report or action. Built on n8n and modern LLMs, connected to the tools you already use.

i.One agent, one job
Each agent has a clear role, its own tools and its own instructions – easier to test, cheaper to run.
ii.Data flows by itself
An orchestrator routes tasks, merges results and decides what happens next – no copy-and-paste between tools.
iii.Humans stay in the loop
Approvals, logs and limits are built in. You see every step and decide where an agent may act on its own.
