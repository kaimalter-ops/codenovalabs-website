Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains which personal data we process when you visit codenovalabs.net and why. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run this website.

1. Controller

CodeNova Labs LLC, 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA
Email: info@ubxplanner.com

2. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted by Vercel Inc., 440 N Barranca Ave #4133, Covina, CA 91723, USA. When you open a page, the server automatically processes technical data such as your IP address, date and time of the request, the page requested, browser type and operating system. This is necessary to deliver the website securely and to detect misuse.

For visitors from the EU/EEA, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure and reliable website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Data may be processed in the USA; transfers are based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or standard contractual clauses. Log data is deleted after a short period unless it is needed to investigate security incidents.

3. No cookies, no tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising tools on this website. Fonts are hosted on our own server, so no data is sent to third-party font providers.

4. Contact form and email

If you contact us via the form or by email, we process the details you provide (name, email address, company if given, topic and message) to answer your request. The legal basis is your request and, where applicable, the preparation of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We delete your data once your request has been fully handled, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Form submissions are transmitted encrypted to our own automation server (n8n), which forwards them to us by email. The server is operated by us and hosted with Oracle Cloud Infrastructure (Oracle Corporation) as our processor. To prevent abuse, the IP address of the sender is kept in temporary memory for up to one hour and is not stored permanently.

[Details on the tool used to receive form submissions will be added before the form goes live.]

5. External links

Our website links to our products (e.g. myinsta.at and ubxplanner.com). These are only loaded when you click a link; the privacy policies of the respective websites then apply.

6. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, and to data portability. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. To exercise your rights, contact us at the address above.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when our website or legal requirements change. The current version is always available on this page.
