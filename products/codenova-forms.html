04 — faq

Good questions.

Does it work with my website?

Yes, as long as you can add a bit of HTML to a page – hand-coded sites, most website builders and CMS systems. The form picks up your fonts and colours automatically.

Where are messages stored?

In your CodeNova inbox, hosted in the EU. Emails are sent through our mail provider. Messages are deleted automatically after 12 months, or earlier if you delete them.

What about privacy and GDPR?

The form sets no cookies and doesn’t track visitors. Link your privacy policy and a consent checkbox is added. We process messages on your behalf and provide a data processing agreement.

What happens when bots find my form?

Hidden trap fields, a minimum fill-in time and limits per sender stop most bots silently. For extra protection, Cloudflare Turnstile can be switched on – no picture puzzles.

Can I have more than one form?

The Forms plan includes one form. CodeNova Blog Pro includes three, Studio as many as you like – together with the full blog tool.
