02 — how it works

Write, approve, publish. That’s it.

the editor

A calm place to write.

Headlines, quotes, lists, links and images – everything a good article needs, nothing it doesn’t. Your work is saved automatically while you type.

  • Cover images, automatically optimised
  • Author profiles with photo and short bio
  • Categories, short teaser and SEO fields
  • Full-screen preview before anything goes live
on your website

Readers never leave your site.

Every article opens right on your own website – with your header, your navigation and your footer. Author boxes with photo and bio give your writers a face.

  • Overview with category filters and “load more”
  • Article pages with reading time and author box
  • Page title, description and structured data for Google
your brand

Your fonts. Your colours. Everywhere.

Upload the fonts of your website and pick your colours once. The editor and preview follow your brand – and on your website, posts automatically pick up your existing styles.

  • Custom font files (WOFF2, WOFF, TTF, OTF)
  • Colours for text, headings and accents
  • One short snippet to embed the blog on any website

04 — faq

Good questions.

Do I need a new website?

No. CodeNova Blog works with the website you already have. We add one short snippet to your blog page – your posts then appear right there, in your design.

Does it work with my website builder?

It works with any website where you can add a bit of HTML – hand-coded sites, most website builders and CMS systems. If you’re unsure, just ask us.

Who can write posts?

Anyone you invite. Authors write and submit, editors review and publish, and owners manage the team and the design. Several people can even write under one shared author profile.

Which languages does the editor support?

The editor will be available in English and German at launch. Your posts can of course be written in any language.

When can I book a plan?
