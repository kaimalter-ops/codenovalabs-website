04 — faq

Good questions.

Who owns my images?

You do. We only use your images to run your website, galleries and uploads. We don’t sell them and we don’t train AI with them. You can download and delete everything at any time.

Where are my originals stored?

In private storage at Cloudflare R2 with EU data location. Originals stay untouched, GPS data included. They only leave when you send them to a stock agency – then without GPS and serial numbers.

How does the upload to stock agencies work?

You enter your agency login once; it is stored encrypted. Darkroom writes title and keywords into the file and uploads the original via SFTP or FTP. You submit it in the agency’s portal yourself, as the agencies require.

What happens when my storage is full?

We let you know in good time. You can add storage in 100 GB steps or move to the next plan. Your website keeps running.

Can I move my existing website?

Yes. Images can be uploaded folder by folder, and your domain then points to Darkroom. If you like, we handle the move for you.

Want to show and sell your images?

Tell us briefly what you photograph. We’ll get back to you personally as soon as Darkroom launches.
