04 — faq

Good questions.

Where are my subscribers stored?

In your CodeNova workspace, hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). Emails are sent through our email provider. Your list is yours: export it as CSV anytime, delete it with one click.

What about privacy and GDPR?

Every signup is confirmed by email (double opt-in), and we store the record. Every email has an unsubscribe link and a footer with your imprint. Opens and clicks are not tracked. We process the data on your behalf and provide a data processing agreement.

Can I bring my existing list?

Yes, via CSV import – as long as all subscribers have consented. When importing, you confirm this and state where the list comes from. We don’t accept bought lists: they hurt deliverability for everyone.

What happens when I run out of emails?

We let you know in time. You can wait until next month or move up to the next tier.

Do I need CodeNova Blog?

No, you can book the newsletter on its own. Bundled with blog and forms it costs less – and posts go to your subscribers with one click.

Want to reach your fans directly?

Tell us briefly who you want to reach. We’ll get back to you personally as soon as the newsletter launches.
